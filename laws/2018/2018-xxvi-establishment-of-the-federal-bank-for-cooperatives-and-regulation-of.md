ACT NO. XXVI OF 2018

[18th May, 2018]

An Act to repeal the Establishment of the Federal Bank for Cooperatives and Regulation of Cooperative Banking Act, 1977

WHEREAS it is expedient to repeal the Establishment of the Federal Bank for Cooperatives and Regulation of Cooperative Banking Act, 1977 (IX of 1977) for the purpose hereinafter appearing;

It is hereby enacted as follows:—

#### 1. Short title and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the Establishment of the Federal Bank for Cooperatives and Regulation of Cooperative Banking (Repeal) Act, 2018.

(2) It shall come into force at once.

#### 2. Repeal

The Establishment of the Federal Bank for Cooperatives and Regulation of Cooperative Banking Act, 1977 (IX of 1977), is hereby repealed.
