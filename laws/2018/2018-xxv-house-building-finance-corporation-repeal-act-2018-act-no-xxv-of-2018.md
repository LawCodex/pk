# House Building Finance Corporation (Repeal) Act, 2018 (Act No_XXV of 2018)

THE HOUSE BUILDING FINANCE CORPORATION (REPEAL)

ACT, 2018

CONTENTS

1. Short title and commencement 2. Repeal THE HOUSE BUILDING FINANCE CORPORATION (REPEAL) ACT, 2018

ACT NO. XXV OF 2018

[18th May, 2018] An Act to repeal the House Building Finance Corporation Act, 1952 WHEREAS it is expedient to repeal the House Building Finance Corporation Act, 1952 (XVIII of 1952), for the purposes hereinafter appealing;

It is hereby enacted as follows:—

#### 1. Short title and commencement

_ (1) This Act may be called the House Building Finance Corporation (Repeal) Act, 2018.

(2) It shall come into force at once.

#### 2. Repeal

The House Building Finance Corporation (Repeal) Act, 1952 (XVIII of 1952) is hereby repealed.
