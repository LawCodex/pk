THE JUTE (REPEAL) ORDINANCE, 1983.

ORDINANCE No. XXXI OF 1983

[22nd December, 1983]

An Ordinance to repeal the Jute Ordinance, 1962.

WHEREAS it is expedient to repeal the Jute Ordinance, 1962 (LXXIV of 1962).

AND WHEREAS the President is satisfied that circumstances exist which render it necessary to take immediate action;

NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of the Proclamation of the fifth day of July, 1977, and in exercise of all powers enabling him in that behalf, the President is pleased to make and promulgate the following Ordinance:—

#### 1. Short title and commencement

(1) This Ordinance may be called the Jute (Repeal) Ordinance, 1983.

(2) It shall come into force at once.

#### 2. Repeal

The Jute Ordinance, 1962 (LXXIV of 1962), is hereby repealed.
