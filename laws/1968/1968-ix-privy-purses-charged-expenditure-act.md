An Act to declare the expenditure relating to privy purses of Rulers of certain former States to be a charge on the Central Consolidated Fund

WHEREAS it is expedient to declare the expenditure relating to privy purses of Rulers of certain former States to be a charge on the Central Consolidated Fund;
