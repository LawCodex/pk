1BOMBAY ACT NO.VI OF 1886

[8th February, 1887]

An Act to vest the Port of Karachi in a Trust

Preamble, Whereas it is expedient to vest the Port of Karachi in a true and to provide for the management of the affairs of the said port by trustees; It is enacted as follows:-

I – PRELIMINARY

#### 1. Short Title

This Act may be called the Karachi Port Trust Act, 1886.

#### 2. Definitions

In this Act, unless there be something repugnant in the subject or context:- (1) “Port” means the port of Karachi as defined for the purpose of this Act;

(2) “high-water mark” means a line drawn through the highest points reached by ordinary spring-tides at any season of the year.

(3) “low-water mark” means a line drawn through the lowest points reached by ordinary spring-tides at any season of the year.

(4) “land” includes the bed of the sea below high-water mark, and also things attached to the earth or permanently fastened to anything attached to the earth;

(5) “master” when used in relation to any vessel, means any person having for the time being the charge or control of such vessel;

(6) the word “goods” includes wares and merchandise of every description; 2[(7) “owner” when used in relation to goods includes any consignor, consignee, shipper, agent for shipping, clearing or removing such goods, or agent for the sale or custody of such goods; and when used in relation to any vessel includes any part-owner, charterer, consignee or mortgage, in possession thereof.]

(18.054-5-0) eighteen thousand and fifty-four and annas five only, to be paid as hereinafter mentioned, all that eight-inch water main now laid an extending from the Currie Reservoir to the Travellers' Bungalow at the junction of Preedy and Bunder Roads and all the iron piping connections and all appurtenances to the said main belonging or appertaining thereto.

2. The Trustees shall lay a six-inch main from the point where the said eight-inch main terminates, joining up one with the other, and carry such six-inch main along the Bunder Road as far as the Merewether Clock Tower then across the road into the Port Trust Import Yard along the cart road in front of the Platform Sheds joining the Bunder Road after passing the Custom House on its south side and along the Napier Mole Road to any such point or points at Keamari as the Trustees may now or at any time hereafter deem necessary or expedient.

3. The Trustees shall be at liberty to open up all roads along which the said six-inch main is to be laid to such depth or width as may be reasonably necessary, provided always that any road or portion of road so opened up shall be closed and restored to its original condition as soon as the main has been laid and provided also that the Trustees shall fence the road so opened and take all the necessary precautions to avoid injury etc., to passengers and vehicular traffic. The work of laying the main shall, when commenced, continue without interruption until completion.

4. The pipe shall wherever practicable, be laid at such a depth below the surface of the road that there shall be at least two feet of filling over every portion of the same.

5. During the construction of the main the Engineer of the Municipality shall at all times have access to the works for the purpose of satisfying the Municipality that the laying of the pipes is being satisfactory carried out.

6. The Trustees shall pay to the Municipality the said sum of Rs. 18.054-5-0 herein agreed to be paid one the completion of the six-inch main and when the new 6 annas rate comes into force.

7. During the continuance of this agreement the whole of the said main from the Currie Reservoir to Keamari shall be the exclusive property of the Trustees, and the Trustees shall maintain and keep the same in proper repair and do all renewals add other works necessary for the proper unkeep of the line in such a manner that whenever, during the continuance of this agreement and under the terms thereof or when on the expiration of 30 years the line is taken over by the Municipality it shall be in a thoroughly good and satisfactory condition to fulfil at that time the functions for which it was originally laid. The Port Trust in fact agree to hand over the main in the same satisfactory conditions as the Municipality would reasonably be expected to have maintained had it been their own property. That in case of any dispute between the Municipality and the Port Trust as to the satisfactory condition of the pipes at the time they are taken over by the Municipality, the dispute shall be referred to the Commissioner in Sind for final decision.

11. The Trustees shall pay to the Municipality for all water as go measured by meter at the rate of six annas per one thousand (1,000) gallons; payment to accrue due on the 1st day of each calendar month and to be made as soon as the amount due shall have been determined.

12. The Trustees shall pay in addition to the said rate of six annas for one thousand (1,000) gallons, so long as the present temporary plague tax on water is chargeable to all other consumers, the plague tax of three annas per one thousand (1,000) gallons, and during the continuance of this agreement the Trustees shall be subject in the same way as all other sections of the community, shall be subject to any special tax of like nature to the present plague tax that maybe levied on all consumers of water under the sanction of the Government.

13. The valve communicating between the Currie Reservoir and the said main shall be always kept open, except when for the purposes of cleaning or repairing the Reservoir or for some similar purpose or when it be necessary under extraordinary circumstances in the interest of the public to restrict the supply it be absolutely necessary that it be closed but before so closing it the Municipality shall give at least 24 hours’ notice to the Trustees of their intention so to do. In the event of any difference of opinion between the Port Trust and the Municipality as to weather it is necessary in the interests of the public to close the valve, the question shall be referred to the Commissioner in Sind, whose decision shall be final on the point, but the valve shall in such case not be closed until the Commissioner in Sind's decision is given.

14. During the continuance of this agreement the Municipality shall not make any connection or branch to or from any part of the said main, nor shall the Municipality do or suffer to be done anything whereby the said main or any part thereof shall be injured or exposed to injury, or whereby the free flow of water from the Currie Reservoir (subject to the provision made in clause 13) into and through the whole length of the said main to Keamari shall be obstructed or in any way impeded.

SCHEDULE D.—contd.

15. This agreement shall, unless previously terminated as hereinafter provided, continue in force for the term of 30 years commencing from such date as the Trustees shall commence to use the said main for the purpose or drawing water therefrom at Keamari.

16. On the termination of this agreement, by efflux of time the whole of the said main and all its appurtenances shall become the absolute property of the Municipality.

17. The Municipality may at any time, by giving three months’ notice in writing of its intention so to do, terminate this agreement and take over the whole of the said main with all its appurtenances on payment of its value at the time of taking it over. This value shell calculated on the basis of provisions of clause 16 hereof. i.e., the price to be paid shall bear the same portion to the original cost of the main (viz., the price under clause 1 and the cost of extension incurred under clause 2 hereof) as the unexpired portion of 30 years to 30 years.

18. So soon as the said main and its appurtenances have been taken over by the Municipality under provisions herein contained, the Engineer to the Municipality and all necessary Assistants and workmen shall at all times have access to the Import Yard or other premises belong to the Port Trust in or under which any portion of the said main shall be laid for the purpose of examining, repairing or doing any other necessary work in connection with the said main or any branches thereof. And it shall be lawful for the Municipality to make any branch connections that they may think necessary with the said main, and for that purpose to open up the ground within the Port Trust premises in such places and to such depth and width as may be necessary: Provided always that it shall be lawful for the Trustees, at anytime after the said main shall have been taken over as aforesaid at their own costs in all things, to remove from their premises such portion of the said main as shall be in or under their premises and relay the same in or under Municipal land, such relaying being carried out under the direction and to the satisfaction of the Engineer to the Municipality.

19. From and after the termination of this agreement, whether by the efflux of time or otherwise under the provisions herein contained, the Trustees shall pay the municipality for water if they require to be supplied to them at such rates as shall mutually be agreed on or, failing this as the Municipality with the sanction of the Local Government shall determine.
