ACT NO. VIII OF 2020

[21st February, 2020]

An Act to repeal the President’s Order No. 15 of 1980

WHEREAS it is expedient to repeal the Superior Courts (Court Dress and Mode of Address) Order, 1980 (P.O. No. 15 of 1980);

It is hereby enacted as follows:__

#### 1. Short title and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the Superior Courts (Court Dress and Mode of Address) Order (Repeal) Act, 2020.

(2) It shall come into force at once.

#### 2. Repeal of President’s Order No

15 of 1980.— The Superior Courts (Court Dress and Mode of Address) Order, 1980 (P.O. No. 15 of 1980), is hereby repealed.
