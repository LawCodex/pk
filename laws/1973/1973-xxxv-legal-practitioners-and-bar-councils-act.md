________ THE LEGAL PRACTITIONERS AND BAR COUNCILS ACT, 1973 ACT No. XXXV OF 1973 [22nd February, 1973]

An Act to repeal and, with certain modifications, re-enact the law relating to legal practitioners and bar councils.

WHEREAS it is expedient to repeal and, with certain modifications, re-enact the law relating to legal practitioners and bar councils and to provide for certain incidental and ancillary matters;

It is hereby enacted as follows:—

### CHAPTER I

PRELIMINARY

#### 1. Short title and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the Legal Practitioners and Bar Councils Act, 1973.

(2) It shall come into force at once.

#### [1A. Overriding effect

_The provisions of this Act shall have effect notwithstanding anything inconsistent therewith contained in any other law for the time being in force.]
